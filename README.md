Student guide.
Contained the instructions and assigned tasks. The guide was distributed weekly together with the session reading material. Students completed the tasks individually and uploaded their responses to the virtual learning platform. The responses were reviewed during the intervention sessions to facilitate discussion and provide feedback.
Instructor guide.
Contained the answer key for the assigned tasks. Its purpose was to standardize teaching content and learning outcomes across instructors and their student groups. The guide also included instructions for conducting the practical sessions.
